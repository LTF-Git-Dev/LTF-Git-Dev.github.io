LP uses no cookies

LP is a Mac app, not a website. It uses no cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, analytics or advertising identifiers — none of its own and none from anyone else.

Signing in to Google or Microsoft

When you connect a calendar account, LP asks macOS to show the sign-in page in a private session. Google or Microsoft may set cookies on their own sign-in page while it is open. With Safari as your default browser, that session shares no cookies with Safari and keeps none when it closes (which is why you type your password each time you connect). If another browser is your default, it may ignore the request and keep its usual cookies. LP never reads them either way.

Shared progress pages

A board you share is a Google Doc on docs.google.com, hosted by Google, or a secret gist on gist.github.com, hosted by GitHub. When someone opens it, Google or GitHub may use cookies on its own site as described in Google's cookie policy or GitHub's cookie policy. LP adds no cookies, scripts or trackers to the page.

Exported reports and the user guide

The HTML report from Export Report… is a single file with no scripts, cookies or links to outside resources, so opening it contacts no one. The user guide PDF contains no tracking either.
